Onboard a Partner
Tutoring centres & education organisations — same portal as a school, lighter setup
ℹ️ This creates a Partner Admin account (identical to a School Admin login). They sign in via Staff Login and land in the same School Admin Portal, where they add their own students — who then log in via Student Login, exactly like any school's students. No SA-SAMS, attendance or fee tracking required — they just won't use those tabs.
