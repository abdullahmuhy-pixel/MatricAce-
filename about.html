Our Purpose

Our Mission

MatricAce exists to close the gap between the quality of education available to students at well-resourced schools and those at under-resourced ones. Every feature we build, every question we write, and every AI interaction we design serves one goal: giving every South African matric student a genuine shot at achieving their potential.

How It Started

The Story

MatricAce started with a frustration shared by millions of South African students: too many generic study apps that don't understand the NSC, and too few affordable tools that actually match how the DBE sets exams.

We set out to build something different — something that speaks the language of South African education. Something that a student in a rural school in Limpopo can use just as effectively as one in a private school in Sandton.

Today MatricAce covers 27+ subjects across Grade 10, 11 and 12, with over 10,000 CAPS-aligned practice questions, AI-powered tutoring, full mock exams, smart flashcards, and a complete teacher dashboard system for schools.

The Platform

What We Build

MatricAce is a full-stack educational technology platform covering every aspect of matric preparation.

🧠
AI Study Buddy
An AI tutor powered by Claude (Anthropic) that answers subject questions, explains concepts in plain language, and adapts to each student's level — available 24/7, completely free.
📋
NSC Mock Exams
Full timed paper simulations with DBE-style questions for every major subject. Includes instant feedback, detailed answer explanations, and performance analytics.
⚡
Smart Flashcards & SRS
AI-generated flashcards for every topic with spaced repetition scheduling — the same learning science used by medical students worldwide, now for matric.
🏫
School & Teacher Dashboards
A complete classroom management system for schools — teacher dashboards, live quiz sessions, attendance tracking, gradebooks, and parent reports.
📄
Past Papers (2019–2024)
Full NSC and IEB past question papers with memoranda, directly accessible as PDFs — no searching required.
